If you use a OnePlus phone, your next big update won't be called OxygenOS. OnePlus has moved its phones to **ColorOS 17**, the same software Oppo uses, based on **Android 17**. The OnePlus 15, 15R, 13 and 13s already have it, and the **OnePlus 12, 13R, Open, Nord 6 and Pad 3** are next, with dates now confirmed for November.

<!--more-->

## What's changed: no more OxygenOS

There's no OxygenOS 17. OnePlus India announced in October that its phones will get **ColorOS 17** instead. The company says the switch will speed up updates and improve software quality by sharing one software team with Oppo. Android Headlines notes that outside China, OxygenOS and ColorOS were already almost identical, so for most people the biggest change is the name and the new design.

India is the first market to get the stable update, according to Cashify.

The exact November dates come from the updated schedule reported by Android Headlines on November 5. OnePlus India's original list only gave the month. Updates go out in batches, so your phone may get it a few days after its date.

According to Cashify, the **OnePlus 10 Pro, 10T, 10R, 11R**, the original **OnePlus Pad** and **Pad Go** are not on the list.

## What's new in ColorOS 17

- **New design:** OnePlus calls it "Living Flow Design", with see-through layers, lighting effects and smoother animations.
- **AI features:** tools such as AI Mind Space, Agent Mode and an AI Photo Agent. Not every feature will come to every phone.
- **AI Call Guard:** warns you about possible fraud calls, which is useful in India, where scam calls are common.
- **Performance:** OnePlus claims faster app launches and lower rendering load. These are the company's own lab figures.

> **Tip:** Back up your phone and charge it to at least 50% before you install a big update like this. Download it only from **Settings > System & Updates > Software update**. There's no "OxygenOS 17" file, so any website offering one is fake.

## How to check for the update

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Go to **System & Updates > Software update**.
3. If the update shows up, tap **Download and install**, then restart when asked.

If you don't see it yet, wait a few days. OnePlus releases updates in batches.

## The bottom line

OnePlus phones are switching from OxygenOS to **ColorOS 17**, based on Android 17. The **OnePlus 13R** got it from November 5, and the **OnePlus 12 (November 17), Pad 3 (November 18), Open (November 24) and Nord 6 and Nord CE6 (November 26)** are next. The 12R, 11 and Nord 5 follow in December, and older Nord phones in early 2027. When it arrives, back up your phone and install it from Settings.
