## 1. Redmi Watch Move: best under ₹2,500

The Redmi Watch Move launched in April 2025 at ₹1,999, and that is still roughly what it costs on Flipkart and Croma (Amazon lists it a little higher, around ₹2,200). For that money you get a 1.85-inch AMOLED screen with up to 600 nits of brightness and an always-on mode, Bluetooth calling, and a claimed battery life of up to 14 days.

It uses Xiaomi's Mi Fitness app for heart rate, SpO2 and sleep tracking, and it's rated IP68 for dust and water. The main thing missing is built-in GPS, so if you run outdoors and want an accurate route map, your phone has to come along.

**Best for:** first-time smartwatch buyers, students, and anyone who mainly wants notifications, calls and step counts without spending much.

## 2. realme Watch S2: best round watch for daily wear

If you'd like something that looks more like a regular watch, the realme Watch S2 has a round 1.43-inch AMOLED screen and a stainless steel body. It launched in mid-2024 at ₹4,999, but it now sells for around ₹3,300 on Flipkart, which makes it much better value.

realme claims up to 20 days of battery, or about 14 days in standard use. It has Bluetooth calling, IP68 protection, over 110 sports modes, and a ChatGPT-powered AI voice assistant, and it pairs with the realme Link app. Like the Redmi Watch Move, there's no built-in GPS.

**Best for:** office use and anyone who prefers a classic round look with long battery life.

## 3. Redmi Watch 5 Lite: best overall under ₹5,000

The Redmi Watch 5 Lite gets the most right for the money. It has a big 1.96-inch AMOLED display at up to 600 nits, Bluetooth calling with dual-mic noise reduction, and a claimed battery life of up to 18 days in typical use (12 days with heavy use).

The big upgrade over cheaper watches is **built-in GPS** that works with five satellite systems, so you can track runs, walks and rides without carrying your phone. It also has 5ATM water resistance (good for swimming), over 160 sports modes, Alexa built in, and works with both Android and iPhone. It launched at ₹3,999, which is again what it costs on Amazon now.

**Best for:** most people. It's the safest all-round pick in this budget, especially if you walk, run or cycle outdoors.

## 4. realme Watch 5: biggest screen with GPS

The realme Watch 5 is the newest watch here. It launched in India in December 2025 at ₹4,499 and now sells for around ₹4,000 on Flipkart and realme's own store. It has the largest screen in this list, a 1.97-inch AMOLED at up to 600 nits, and **built-in GPS** that works with five satellite systems.

realme claims up to 16 days of battery in normal use and up to 20 days in a lighter mode. It also has Bluetooth calling, NFC and IP68 dust and water resistance. Its rating is IP68 rather than 5ATM, so the Redmi Watch 5 Lite is the safer choice if you want to swim with your watch.

**Best for:** people who want built-in GPS and a big, easy-to-read screen, and who prefer realme's app or already use a realme phone.

## 5. CMF Watch Pro 2: best design with GPS

Nothing's sub-brand CMF makes one of the better-looking budget watches. The Watch Pro 2 has a round 1.32-inch AMOLED screen at 466 x 466 resolution and up to 620 nits, with always-on display support. It includes built-in GPS, Bluetooth calling, IP68 protection and up to 11 days of claimed battery life.

It's currently around ₹4,350 on Croma, so it's the most expensive watch here. You pay a bit extra for the design and the CMF Watch app, which is cleaner than many budget apps. The screen is smaller than the Redmi Watch 5 Lite's, which matters if you read a lot of messages on your wrist.

**Best for:** people who want a stylish round watch that can also track outdoor workouts accurately.

## What to check before you buy

**Built-in GPS or connected GPS.** Many budget watches say "GPS" but mean connected GPS, which borrows your phone's location. Only true built-in GPS tracks a run when your phone stays at home. Of the five here, the Redmi Watch 5 Lite, realme Watch 5 and CMF Watch Pro 2 have it.

**AMOLED vs LCD.** Almost every good watch under ₹5,000 now uses AMOLED, which looks sharper and is easier to read outdoors. Avoid LCD (often listed as TFT) models in this budget unless they are much cheaper.

**Battery claims.** Brands measure battery with light use. Turn on always-on display, take a few calls daily and use GPS, and a "14-day" watch may last about half of that.

**Health readings are estimates.** Heart rate, SpO2 and sleep data from budget watches are useful for spotting trends, not for medical decisions. None of these watches is a medical device.

**The app matters.** You'll use the companion app every day, so make sure it supports your phone. Check the store listing for iPhone support before you buy; most budget watches work with both Android and iPhone, though iPhone users usually get fewer features, such as quick replies.

> **Tip:** Smartwatch prices in India drop sharply during big sales. If the watch you want is close to its launch price, check Amazon and Flipkart again during the next sale event, as discounts of ₹500–₹1,000 are common in this segment.
