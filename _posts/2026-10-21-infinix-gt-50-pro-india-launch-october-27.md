Infinix has now launched the **GT 50 Pro** in India at **₹69,999** for 12GB + 256GB, sold as a bundle with gaming accessories. That's far above the ₹35,000–₹40,000 that pre-launch reports guessed. The first sale is on **October 30 at 12 PM** on Flipkart. The phone has a MediaTek Dimensity 8400 Ultimate chip, a 144Hz AMOLED screen, visible liquid cooling and a 6,500mAh battery.

<!--more-->

Here are the official price, offers and specs for India, and how it compares with cheaper gaming phones.

## Price and availability in India

Infinix has announced one variant for India, and it is only sold as a bundle with gaming accessories. Reports say there is no phone-only option at launch.

| Variant | India price |
|---|---|
| 12GB + 256GB | ₹69,999 (with gaming accessories) |

- **First sale:** October 30, 2026, at 12 PM on Flipkart. Pre-orders are open now on Flipkart and Infinix's India website.
- **Colours:** Black Abyss (with green cooling accents) and Red Blaze (with red accents)
- **In the bundle:** gaming accessories that Infinix values at ₹24,999, including a gaming controller, the GT NX Station dock, a cooling fan with charging, and a magnetic cover. Reports list the items slightly differently, so check the Flipkart listing for the exact box contents.
- **Bank offer:** ₹5,000 instant discount on credit cards from SBI, ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank, Axis Bank and Yes Bank, which brings the effective price to ₹64,999.
- **Other offers:** no-cost EMI, up to 6,999 Infinix reward points (only for vouchers on Infinix's own store) and a JioGames Pro Pass for ₹1.

For reference, last year's GT 30 Pro launched in India at ₹24,999 for 8GB + 256GB, so the GT 50 Pro costs almost three times as much. Reports link the jump to the global rise in memory chip prices and the accessory bundle. The GT 30 Pro now sells for around ₹31,999 on online stores (October 2026), according to price trackers.

## What's new

The biggest change from the GT 30 Pro is the chip. The Dimensity 8400 Ultimate replaces last year's Dimensity 8350 Ultimate, and Infinix claims up to 144fps in supported games such as Free Fire MAX and Call of Duty: Mobile.

The battery also grows from 5,500mAh to 6,500mAh, while charging stays at 45W wired and 30W wireless. On the camera side, the 108MP main sensor of the GT 30 Pro makes way for a 50MP main camera with optical image stabilisation (OIS), which helps with steadier low-light photos and video.

The design is the other headline. Infinix calls it "Cyber Mecha": a textured back with a window that shows the liquid cooling channels, plus four RGB strips with 14 lighting effects that react to calls, games and music. The shoulder triggers can be set up for aiming, firing, zooming or opening apps.

Infinix is also bringing gaming accessories to India: the **GT NX Controller** (a clip-on controller that can split into two Bluetooth handles), the **GT NX Station** (a dock for playing on a TV or monitor at up to 4K 60Hz), and the **GT MagCharge Cooler 2.0** (a magnetic cooling fan with 15W wireless charging). In India, the accessories come bundled with the phone at launch rather than being sold separately.

## Who should consider it

The GT 50 Pro is for mobile gamers who want the full kit (shoulder triggers, a controller, a TV dock and a cooling fan) in one box. At ₹69,999, though, it costs about as much as some flagships, so it only makes sense if you'll actually use the accessories. Camera-first buyers should look elsewhere.

If you mainly want gaming performance, these cost about half as much (prices at the time of writing):

- **OPPO K13 Turbo 5G** (around ₹33,999 at the time of writing): Dimensity 8450 chip, a 7,000mAh battery and 80W charging, plus a built-in cooling fan.
- **Motorola Edge 70 Pro 5G** (around ₹39,799 at the time of writing): Dimensity 8500 Extreme chip, a 6,500mAh battery and faster 90W charging.

> **Tip:** Price out what you'd actually use. If you'd never connect the dock to a TV or use the controller, a ₹35,000–₹40,000 phone like the OPPO K13 Turbo plus a ₹1,500–₹2,000 clip-on cooling fan gets you most of the gaming experience for far less.
