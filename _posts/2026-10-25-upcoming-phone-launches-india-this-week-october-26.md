It's a quiet week for phone launches in India. Only one new phone has a confirmed India launch date, the Infinix GT 50 Pro on October 27, and Apple's "Pro and Tell" event follows a day later. Here's what's confirmed, plus three big flagships that are officially coming to India but don't have a date yet.

<!--more-->

## This week at a glance

| Date | What's launching | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesday, October 27 | Infinix GT 50 Pro (gaming phone) | Launched at ₹69,999; first sale October 30 on Flipkart |
| Wednesday, October 28 | Apple "Pro and Tell" event (Macs and iPads expected, no phones) | Event confirmed by Apple; products not confirmed |
| No date yet | iQOO 16, Xiaomi 18 Pro, Motorola Signature 27 | India launch confirmed, date and price not announced |

Apart from the Infinix GT 50 Pro, prices are still unannounced, so be careful with "India price" claims you see on social media before launch day.

## Infinix GT 50 Pro: launched October 27

Infinix launched the GT 50 Pro in India on Tuesday, October 27, and it's sold on Flipkart. It's a gaming phone with a visible liquid cooling system on the back, RGB light strips and pressure-sensitive shoulder triggers. It comes in two colours, Black Abyss and Red Blaze.

**Update, October 27:** The GT 50 Pro is now official in India at **₹69,999** for its only variant, 12GB + 256GB, sold as a bundle with gaming accessories that Infinix values at ₹24,999. There's a ₹5,000 instant discount on select bank credit cards, and the first sale is on **October 30 at 12 PM** on Flipkart.

That's far above the ₹35,000–₹40,000 that pre-launch reports guessed. We've updated our [Infinix GT 50 Pro launch post](/infinix-gt-50-pro-india-launch-october-27/) with the full price, offers and specs.

## Apple's "Pro and Tell" event: October 28

Apple has confirmed an event called "Pro and Tell" on Wednesday, October 28, at 10 AM Pacific time, which is **10:30 PM IST** in India. Recent reports say it will be a hands-on session for invited journalists in Los Angeles rather than a normal keynote, and Apple hasn't announced a public livestream.

No new iPhones are expected. Reports point to redesigned MacBook Pro models with OLED touchscreens, an updated iMac and a new iPad mini, but Apple hasn't confirmed any products. India prices usually appear on Apple's India website soon after an announcement. Our [Apple event preview](/apple-pro-and-tell-event-october-28-what-to-expect/) has the details.

## Confirmed for India, date not announced

These three flagships were confirmed for India at India Mobile Congress (IMC) 2026 in early October. None has a launch date or India price yet.

- **iQOO 16:** iQOO India's CEO, Nipun Marya, confirmed it's coming to India. It launched in China on September 29 with a Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 chip, an 8,400mAh battery and three 50MP rear cameras, starting at CNY 5,999 (about ₹86,000). Reports expect the India launch around December or early 2027, but iQOO hasn't confirmed timing.
- **Xiaomi 18 Pro:** Xiaomi confirmed the India launch on stage at Qualcomm's Snapdragon event at IMC. The China model has a small 2.7-inch display on the back, two 200MP cameras (main and telephoto) tuned with Leica, and a 7,000mAh battery with 100W charging. Reports suggest it may arrive by the end of December.
- **Motorola Signature 27:** Motorola's India marketing head confirmed it at IMC. Reports say it uses a Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 chip and a 200MP periscope telephoto camera, and Motorola promises seven years of Android updates. Some reports expect a November launch, but there's no official date. **Update, October 30:** Motorola's India site now says "coming this November". See our [Motorola Signature 27 expected price and specs](/motorola-signature-27-india-launch-november-expected-price-specs/).

What about the **OnePlus 16** and the **vivo X500** series? Both have launched in China, and reports expect them in India soon, but neither brand has confirmed an India launch date yet.

## Who should wait

If you're about to buy a mid-range gaming phone, note that the GT 50 Pro launched at ₹69,999, so it isn't a mid-range option this time. If you're spending ₹80,000 or more on a flagship, it's worth knowing that three new ones are officially on the way, even if they're weeks away.
