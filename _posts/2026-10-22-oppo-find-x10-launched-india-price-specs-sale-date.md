OPPO has launched the **Find X10** and **Find X10 Pro Max** in India, with prices starting at **₹1,39,999**. The Pro Max is the headline phone, with three 200MP rear cameras tuned with Hasselblad and an 8,000mAh battery, and both phones go on sale on **October 29**.

<!--more-->

Here are the official Indian prices, what you get for the money, and who should actually consider spending this much on an OPPO phone.

## Price and availability in India

| Model | RAM + storage | India price |
|---|---|---|
| Find X10 | 12GB + 256GB | ₹1,39,999 |
| Find X10 | 12GB + 512GB | ₹1,69,999 |
| Find X10 Pro Max | 12GB + 512GB | ₹1,99,999 |
| Hasselblad Teleconverter Kit (optional) | — | ₹24,999 |

- **Sale date:** Thursday, October 29, 2026
- **Where to buy:** OPPO's online store, Flipkart and Amazon
- **Colours:** Glacier Blue, Mist Grey and Glow Coral for the Find X10; Luna White and Titanium Grey for the Find X10 Pro Max
- **Pre-orders:** open now on OPPO's online store, Flipkart and Amazon
- **Launch offers (as reported in October 2026):** a 12% instant bank discount (up to ₹16,799 on the Find X10 and up to ₹23,999 on the Pro Max), an exchange bonus of up to ₹8,000, no-cost EMI from around ₹5,833 a month (Find X10) and ₹8,333 a month (Pro Max), and a free gift box with OPPO Enco Air5 Pro earbuds and a magnetic case. OPPO hasn't listed the eligible banks in the reports we checked, so confirm the card offers on the product page before you pay.

These prices are much higher than last year. The Find X9 started at ₹74,999 and the Find X9 Pro cost ₹1,09,999, so the new series has moved firmly into the ₹1.4 lakh to ₹2 lakh bracket.

OPPO sells a smaller 7,150mAh battery version of the series in some markets, such as Europe, but the Indian models get the full 8,000mAh battery.

## What's new

**Cameras.** The Find X10 Pro Max uses 200MP sensors for all three rear cameras: the main, the ultra-wide and the 3x zoom. Most flagships use a high-resolution sensor only for the main camera and sometimes the zoom, so this is an unusual setup. By default, photos are saved at a smaller size to keep files manageable, and you can switch to full resolution when you want more detail. The telephoto can also focus as close as 10cm, which lets it double as a macro camera.

**Teleconverter kit.** The optional Hasselblad Teleconverter Kit clips onto the phone for extra optical reach. It costs ₹24,999 and goes on sale on the same day as the phones.

**Battery and charging.** Both phones move up to an 8,000mAh silicon-carbon battery, compared with 7,500mAh on last year's Find X9 Pro. Charging stays at 80W wired, and there is 50W wireless charging (the wireless charger is sold separately).

**Chips.** The Pro Max gets MediaTek's new 2nm Dimensity 9600 Pro, while the regular Find X10 uses the Dimensity 9600M. Both have IP66, IP68 and IP69 ratings for dust and water protection.

## Who should consider it

The **Find X10 Pro Max** makes sense if photography, especially zoom and wide-angle shots, matters more to you than anything else, and you want a battery that comfortably lasts a full day. The **Find X10** offers most of the same camera ideas and the same battery in a more compact body, for ₹60,000 less.

At these prices, the competition is tough:

- **Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra:** a built-in S Pen, a strong zoom camera and Samsung's long software support. It often sells below its launch price during sales.
- **Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max:** the obvious choice if you already use a Mac, iPad or Apple Watch.

> **Tip:** Don't book on day one just because of the launch buzz. Wait for the October 29 sale to see the final bank and exchange offers, then compare the effective price with the Galaxy S26 Ultra's current street price. A ₹1.4 lakh or ₹2 lakh phone needs to win on your actual price, not the sticker price.

## The bottom line

The OPPO Find X10 series is now official in India at ₹1,39,999 for the Find X10 and ₹1,99,999 for the Find X10 Pro Max, with sales starting on October 29 on OPPO's store, Flipkart and Amazon. On paper, the Pro Max is one of the most camera-focused phones you can buy, with three 200MP cameras, an 8,000mAh battery and full IP69 protection. The price is steep, though, so wait for the sale-day offers and compare carefully with Samsung and Apple before you decide.
