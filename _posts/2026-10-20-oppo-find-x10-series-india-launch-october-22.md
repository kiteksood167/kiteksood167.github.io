OPPO has officially confirmed that its new flagship phones, the **Find X10** and **Find X10 Pro Max**, will launch in India on **Thursday, October 22, 2026, at 4:45 PM IST**. Both phones are already on sale in China, so we already know a lot about the hardware, including the headline feature: three 200MP rear cameras on the Pro Max.

<!--more-->

Here's what OPPO has confirmed for India, what we know from the China launch, and what is still unknown.

## Price and availability in India

OPPO has **not announced India prices yet**. They will be revealed at the launch event. Some price figures are going around online, but they come from leaks, so we are not treating them as facts.

For reference, here is what the Pro Max costs in China, along with the confirmed India colours:

| Model | Variant | China price | India price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Find X10 Pro Max | 12GB + 256GB | CNY 6,799 | Not announced yet |
| Find X10 Pro Max | 12GB + 512GB | CNY 7,499 | Not announced yet |
| Find X10 Pro Max | 16GB + 512GB | CNY 7,999 | Not announced yet |
| Find X10 Pro Max | 16GB + 1TB | CNY 8,999 | Not announced yet |
| Find X10 | To be confirmed | — | Not announced yet |

China prices don't convert directly to Indian prices, because of import duties and GST. As a guide, last year's Find X9 Pro launched in India at ₹1,09,999 for 16GB + 512GB, and the Find X9 started at ₹74,999.

- **Launch date:** October 22, 2026, 4:45 PM IST
- **Sale date and stores:** not confirmed yet. Last year's Find X9 phones were sold on OPPO's website, Amazon, Flipkart and in shops, but OPPO hasn't confirmed the stores for the X10 yet.
- **Colours:** Luna White and Titanium Grey for the Find X10 Pro Max; Glacier Blue, Mist Grey and Glow Coral for the Find X10.
- **Pre-launch offer:** OPPO's India website has a ₹9 "Privilege Pack". It gives a ₹1,000 coupon for the OPPO Pad Mini and a ₹500 coupon for the Enco X4 earbuds, plus chances to win prizes such as the Find X10 itself. Most of the advertised value is from prize draws, not guaranteed discounts, so read the terms before paying.

OPPO is expected to show the Pad Mini tablet and Enco X4 earbuds at the same event, based on its India store listing.

## Key specifications

These are the Find X10 Pro Max specs from its China launch, confirmed by multiple reports. OPPO hasn't yet said whether the Indian model is exactly the same, but flagship phones usually don't change much between markets.

| Spec | Find X10 Pro Max |
|---|---|
| Display | 6.78-inch LTPO OLED, 2772 × 1272, up to 144Hz |
| Processor | MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro (2nm) |
| RAM and storage | 12GB or 16GB RAM, 256GB to 1TB storage |
| Rear cameras | 200MP main + 200MP ultra-wide + 200MP 3x periscope telephoto, Hasselblad tuning |
| Front camera | 50MP |
| Battery | 8,000mAh |
| Charging | 80W wired, plus wireless charging |
| Durability | IP66, IP68 and IP69 dust and water resistance |
| Software | ColorOS 17 based on Android 17 |

The regular **Find X10** is a smaller phone with a 6.59-inch LTPO screen and a MediaTek Dimensity 9600M chip. Its rear cameras are a 200MP main sensor, a 50MP ultra-wide and a 200MP telephoto, and it also has a 50MP selfie camera.

## What's new

The biggest change is the camera. The Find X9 Pro already had a 200MP telephoto, but the Find X10 Pro Max uses 200MP sensors for **all three** rear cameras: the main camera, the ultra-wide and the 3x zoom. In theory, that means more detail in your wide-angle and zoom shots, not just the main camera. OPPO has also confirmed that a Hasselblad Teleconverter Kit, an add-on lens for longer zoom, will work with the 200MP periscope camera.

The battery also gets bigger, from 7,500mAh on the Find X9 Pro to 8,000mAh. The Dimensity 9500 chip is replaced by the new 2nm Dimensity 9600 Pro. The phone is also rated IP66, IP68 and IP69, so it's well protected against dust, water and even high-pressure water jets.
## Who should consider it

If you mostly care about cameras, especially zoom, and want a phone that easily lasts a full day, the Find X10 Pro Max looks like one of the most interesting flagships of the year on paper. The regular Find X10 should suit buyers who want the same software and most of the camera features in a smaller, cheaper phone.

Close rivals in India will include:

- **Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra** (launched at ₹1,39,999): a built-in S Pen and a long promise of software updates.
- **Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max**: for anyone already using a Mac, iPad or other Apple devices.

> **Tip:** Don't rush to buy on launch day. Flagship phones in India often get bank card discounts and exchange bonuses in the first sale week. Compare the final price after offers with the Galaxy S26 Ultra's current street price, which is often well below its launch price.

## The bottom line

The OPPO Find X10 series arrives in India on October 22 at 4:45 PM IST. On paper, the Pro Max is a strong package with three 200MP cameras, an 8,000mAh battery and full IP69 protection. The big unknown is the price. Wait for the official Indian prices, sale date and bank offers on launch day before you decide.
