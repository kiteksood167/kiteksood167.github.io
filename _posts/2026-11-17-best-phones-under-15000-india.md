Finding a good phone under ₹15,000 is harder in late 2026 than it was a year ago. Memory chips have become expensive, and many popular budget phones have quietly moved above ₹15,000. The good news: four phones still give you real value at this price, each strong in a different way, so the right pick depends on what you care about most.

## Why fewer phones fit under ₹15,000 now

Through 2026, brands have raised prices on budget phones because RAM and storage chips cost more. The Lava price hike of up to ₹3,000 that started on November 15 is only the latest one. According to 91mobiles' price-hike tracker, the Redmi 15C (4GB + 128GB) went from ₹12,499 to ₹16,999, and the Vivo T4x (6GB + 128GB) from ₹13,999 to ₹16,999. Phones that were easy recommendations at ₹13,000 now cost ₹17,000 or more.

## 1. POCO M7 Pro 5G: best display and camera

The POCO M7 Pro 5G launched in December 2024 at ₹14,999 for the 6GB + 128GB model, and that is still its Flipkart price today, which is rare in a year of price hikes.

Its biggest strength is the screen. You get a 6.67-inch **Full HD+ AMOLED** panel with a 120Hz refresh rate, up to 2,100 nits peak brightness and Gorilla Glass 5. Most phones at this price use HD+ LCD screens, so text, videos and photos look noticeably sharper and more colourful here.

The main camera is also a step above the usual budget setup: a 50MP Sony LYT-600 sensor with **optical image stabilisation (OIS)**, which helps reduce blur in low light and in videos. There's a 20MP selfie camera, an in-display fingerprint sensor, an IR blaster and an IP64 rating for dust and splashes. The 5,110mAh battery charges at 45W.

The catch is software. POCO promised two Android upgrades and four years of security updates from Android 14, so most of the OS upgrades are already used up. If you keep phones for four years or more, look at the Samsung below.

**Best for:** anyone who watches a lot of video or wants the best photos under ₹15,000.

## 2. Infinix Note 50x 5G+: best performance

If speed and gaming matter most, the Infinix Note 50x 5G+ is the pick. Its **MediaTek Dimensity 7300 Ultimate** is a 4nm chip and the most powerful processor in this list, clearly ahead of the Dimensity 6300 and 6400 chips that most phones under ₹15,000 use.

The 8GB + 128GB model sells for around ₹14,999 on Flipkart. The cheaper 6GB version is often out of stock, so check before you decide. You get a 5,500mAh battery with 45W charging, IP64 dust and splash resistance, MIL-STD-810H durability certification, Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.4.

The trade-off is the display. It's a 6.67-inch **HD+ LCD** with 120Hz and a peak brightness of 672 nits, so it is less sharp and harder to read in harsh sunlight than the POCO's AMOLED. Infinix's XOS software is also busier than stock Android, and we couldn't find a clear update promise from the brand.

**Best for:** students and gamers who want the most performance for the money.

## 3. Samsung Galaxy F16 5G: best for long-term updates

The Galaxy F16 5G is the cheapest phone here at around **₹12,499** for the 4GB + 128GB model on Flipkart, and it has one feature none of the others match: Samsung promised **six generations of Android upgrades and six years of security updates**. It launched with Android 15 in March 2025 and has already received One UI 8.5, so it should keep getting updates until around 2031.

It also has a 6.7-inch Full HD+ Super AMOLED screen (90Hz), a 50MP triple camera, a 13MP selfie camera, NFC with Samsung Wallet tap-to-pay, IP54 splash resistance and a slim 7.9mm body.

What you give up is speed. The Dimensity 6300 is fine for WhatsApp, YouTube, UPI and social media, but heavy games will run at lower settings, and 4GB of RAM fills up quickly. If your budget allows, the 6GB + 128GB version, at around ₹13,999, is the better buy. Charging tops out at 25W.

**Best for:** parents, first-time smartphone users and anyone who keeps a phone for four to five years.

> **Tip:** On a budget phone, check the storage before the RAM. A 64GB phone fills up fast once WhatsApp media and photos pile up. If you pick a 64GB model, make sure it has a microSD slot (the Moto G37 and POCO M7 Pro do) and buy a card early.

## 4. Moto G37: best clean software and durability

Launched in May 2026 at ₹13,999, the Moto G37 now sells for around **₹14,499** on Flipkart for the 4GB + 64GB model. It's the newest phone here and has the most polished build for the price.

You get Android 16 with Motorola's clean, close-to-stock interface, **stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos**, a 3.5mm headphone jack, Gorilla Glass 7i, an IP64 rating and MIL-STD-810H certification. The 6.7-inch HD+ LCD runs at 120Hz with up to 1,050 nits peak brightness. The Dimensity 6400 chip is a small step up from the Dimensity 6300 and handles everyday tasks smoothly.

There are some clear limits. Storage is only 64GB (UFS 2.2, expandable by microSD), the 5,200mAh battery supports 20W charging but ships with just a 10W charger, and Motorola promises only **one Android upgrade and three years of security updates**. The 50MP camera is a single sensor without OIS.

The Moto G37 Power version adds a 7,000mAh battery and 30W charging, but its listed prices have mostly been above ₹15,000 in recent months, so it doesn't make this list.

**Best for:** people who want simple, clean software, loud speakers and a tough body.
