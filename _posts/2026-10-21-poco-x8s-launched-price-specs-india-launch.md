Poco has unveiled the Poco X8s, a compact phone with a 6.35-inch OLED screen, the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chip, a big 6,330mAh battery and 100W fast charging. It has gone up for pre-order in Vietnam first, with deliveries starting in early November. Poco hasn't announced an India launch yet. Here's what is confirmed so far.

<!--more-->

## Price and availability

| Variant | Price in Vietnam | Approx. in US dollars |
|---|---|---|
| 8GB RAM + 256GB | VND 11,500,000 | around $437 |
| 12GB RAM + 512GB | VND 14,000,000 | around $532 |

- **Where:** pre-orders through Xiaomi's official store on Shopee Vietnam
- **Deliveries:** expected between November 6 and 9
- **Launch offers:** launch vouchers bring the effective price down to around $313 and $387, and early buyers get free Redmi Buds 6 Play earbuds while stocks last

## India launch: not confirmed yet

Poco has **not** announced an India launch date or Indian prices for the X8s. Price-tracking site Smartprix lists it as an upcoming phone with an expected India price of around ₹23,999, but that is an estimate, not an official figure. The phone has passed certifications with a global model number, which usually means more markets are coming, but nothing is official for India yet.

Vietnam prices don't tell us much about Indian pricing, because phone prices often differ a lot between countries. We'll update this post once Poco confirms the India launch.

## What's new

The headline is the size. Most phones today have screens of 6.7 inches or bigger, so a 6.35-inch phone with flagship-style features is unusual, especially from Poco. It's aimed at people who want a phone that's easy to use with one hand.

The battery is also large for a phone this small. 6,330mAh is more than many full-size phones carry, and 100W charging should refill it quickly. Add IP68 water resistance and a 50MP telephoto camera for zoom shots, and the X8s packs more than you'd usually find in a compact mid-range phone.

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 is an upper mid-range chip. It should handle daily use and most games well, though it isn't a flagship chip like the Snapdragon 8 series.

## Who should consider it

If you've been waiting for a small Android phone that doesn't compromise much on battery life or water resistance, the Poco X8s is one to watch, provided Poco brings it to India at a sensible price.
