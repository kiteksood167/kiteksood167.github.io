Motorola has officially launched the **Signature 27** in the US at **$1,599.99**, the highest price it has ever set for a phone in America. It's Motorola's most powerful phone yet, with Qualcomm's **Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6**, a **200MP periscope zoom camera**, a **7,000mAh battery** and **seven years of OS updates**. Pre-orders open on **November 19**, and it goes on sale on **November 26**, the day before Black Friday.

**UK and Europe:** Motorola has teased the Signature 27 on its UK and European websites, but hasn't announced a price or date. A Portuguese retailer briefly listed the 12GB + 512GB model at **€1,594.90**, which Tech Advisor converts to about £1,330. Treat that as a leak until Motorola confirms it.

## What stands out

**The zoom camera.** A 200MP periscope sensor with 3x optical zoom is rare even among flagships. Motorola's 100x "Super Zoom Pro" uses generative AI at the long end, so expect very long shots to be partly AI-reconstructed rather than pure detail.

**Battery and charging.** A 7,000mAh silicon-carbon battery is much bigger than in most US flagships, and 90W wired charging is far faster than what Apple, Google and Samsung offer. You'll need to buy a compatible charger separately to get that speed.

**Software support.** Seven OS upgrades and up to seven years of security patches match what Google and Samsung promise for their flagships. It's also the first Motorola phone to support **GrapheneOS**, the privacy-focused version of Android, which Motorola developed with the GrapheneOS Foundation.

**What's missing.** The screen tops out at 120Hz with 1.5K resolution, not the 2K panel some leaks suggested. And despite Qi2-ready wireless charging, there are no magnets built in, so MagSafe-style accessories need a magnetic case.

## Is it worth $1,599?

That's a lot for a Motorola. You're paying flagship-plus money for the zoom camera, the battery, fast charging and long updates, but Motorola doesn't have Apple's or Samsung's resale value, and US carrier deals haven't been announced yet.

**Consider it if** you want the biggest battery and fastest charging in a top-end phone, you shoot a lot of zoom photos, or you want official GrapheneOS support.

**Skip it if** you want MagSafe accessories, strong trade-in value, or a carrier deal. At this price, wait for the first reviews before you pre-order.
