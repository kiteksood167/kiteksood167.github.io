## 1. OnePlus Nord Buds 3 Pro: best overall under ₹3,000

The Nord Buds 3 Pro launched at ₹3,299 and now sells for around **₹2,800**. That makes them the strongest all-rounder that stays inside a ₹3,000 budget.

OnePlus rates the hybrid ANC at **up to 49dB**, which is enough to cut most bus, metro and fan noise. There's also a transparency mode for when you need to hear announcements. Each bud has a 12.4mm titanium driver and three microphones with AI call noise reduction.

Battery life is rated at up to 12 hours per bud with ANC off (5.5 hours with ANC on), and up to **44 hours with the case**. A 10-minute charge is claimed to give about 11 hours of listening. They're IP55 rated, so sweat and light rain aren't a problem. Codec support is SBC and AAC, which is normal at this price.

**Best for:** daily commuters who want strong ANC without going over ₹3,000.

## 3. CMF Buds Neo: best battery life with ANC

The CMF Buds Neo from Nothing's budget brand launched in August at ₹1,999 and currently sell for around **₹1,800** on Flipkart. They stand out for their design, with a two-tone case and colourful eartips.

The ANC is milder here, at **up to 35dB** with an adaptive mode, so they won't block as much noise as the two picks above. Battery is the big strength: up to 11 hours per bud and **up to 52 hours with the case** with ANC off (30 hours with ANC on). A 10-minute charge adds about 5.5 hours.

You also get Bluetooth 6.0, dual-device connection, four mics and the Nothing X app with a custom EQ. The earbuds are IP54 rated, but the case is only IPX2. The game mode is rated at 120ms, which is slower than the others here, so serious gamers should look elsewhere.

**Best for:** people who forget to charge, and anyone who wants earbuds that look a bit different.

**Best for:** anyone who wants reliable, long-lasting earbuds and isn't bothered by outside noise.

## What to look for under ₹3,000

- **ANC strength:** Brands quote the maximum dB in lab conditions. Treat it as a rough guide: a higher number generally means stronger noise cancelling, but fit matters just as much.
- **Battery with ANC on:** Look at the ANC-on figure, not just the headline number. That's what you'll get on a noisy commute.
- **Fit:** Even the best ANC won't work if the eartips don't seal. Try all the eartip sizes in the box.
- **Game mode:** Under 50ms is good for casual gaming. Higher figures can cause a slight delay between what you see and hear.
- **Codecs:** At this price, AAC and SBC are normal. You don't need LDAC or LHDC unless you stream high-quality music files.
