If you searched "AirPods Pro 3 vs AirPods 4," there's one big change you need to know first: Apple replaced AirPods 4 with AirPods 5 in September 2026, and the new model includes noise cancellation at the old entry-level price. So the real choice this Black Friday is between AirPods Pro 3 and AirPods 5, with AirPods 4 worth buying only as a true clearance deal.

<!--more-->

## The short answer

- **Buy AirPods Pro 3** if you want the strongest noise cancellation, a sealed in-ear fit, the longest battery life, workout heart rate tracking and Apple's hearing health features.
- **Buy AirPods 5** if you hate silicone ear tips, want an open, lightweight fit, and still want decent noise cancellation for less money.
- **Buy AirPods 4** only if you find the ANC version well below the price of AirPods 5. It is now discontinued in the UK, and stock is drying up.

## What happened to AirPods 4?

Apple announced AirPods 5 on September 9, 2026. Both versions of AirPods 5 come with active noise cancellation (ANC), which Apple says removes up to 50% more noise than AirPods 4 with ANC. The cheaper AirPods 5 lists at $129, the same price as the old AirPods 4 *without* ANC, while AirPods 4 with ANC launched at $179.

That makes AirPods 4 hard to recommend at anything close to full price. Some retailers still have stock: in early November, MacRumors' price tracker showed AirPods 4 with ANC at around $145 at B&H, and Macworld listed it at £109 on Amazon UK (marked as discontinued). If you see it for well under $129 (or £119), it's still a decent pair of open-fit earbuds. Otherwise, AirPods 5 is the better buy.

## Fit: the decision most people should start with

The biggest practical difference isn't a spec; it's how the earbuds sit in your ears.

AirPods Pro 3 use ear tips that seal your ear canal. Apple added foam-infused tips and now offers five sizes, which helps people who found older Pro tips loose. A good seal is what makes strong noise cancellation and deeper bass possible. The trade-off is that some people find any in-ear tip uncomfortable or "plugged up" after an hour or two.

AirPods 5 (like AirPods 4) rest in the outer ear with no tips. They feel lighter and more natural, you hear more of your surroundings, and there's nothing to swap or clean. But an open fit can't block noise as well, and they may not stay put for everyone during runs.

> **Tip:** If you've never been able to wear tipless AirPods (they fall out) or you've never liked in-ear tips (they feel blocked), that history matters more than any spec in this article. Buy from a store with an easy return window, and test the fit during the first few days.

## Noise cancellation and sound

On noisy commutes, flights and open offices, AirPods Pro 3 are in a different league. Apple says they cancel twice as much noise as AirPods Pro 2 and up to three times as much as AirPods 5. Reviews from Tom's Guide and BGR also rate the Pro 3's sound quality ahead of AirPods 5.

AirPods 5's ANC is still a useful upgrade. It takes the edge off traffic, fans and engine hum, and Apple's Adaptive Audio and Conversation Awareness work on all three models. Just don't expect open-ear earbuds to shut out a crying baby on a plane.

## Battery life

AirPods Pro 3 last up to 8 hours per charge with ANC on, which covers a long flight or a full workday of on-and-off listening. AirPods 5 give 4 to 5 hours with ANC on. The $149 model with the wireless charging case gets an extra hour per charge compared with the $129 version. AirPods 4 with ANC were rated at up to 4 hours.

All three cases charge over USB-C, and a five-minute top-up gives roughly an hour of listening on AirPods 4 and AirPods 5.

## Extras that may tip the balance

**Workouts.** AirPods Pro 3 have a built-in heart rate sensor. With an iPhone, they can track heart rate and calories during workouts and count toward your Move ring, even without an Apple Watch. Both Pro 3 and AirPods 5 are rated IP57, a step up from AirPods 4's IP54. If you already wear an Apple Watch, the heart rate sensor matters much less. Our [best smartwatches for iPhone](/best-smartwatches-for-iphone/) guide covers that side.

**Hearing health.** AirPods Pro 3 support Apple's hearing health features, including hearing aid mode where it's approved. AirPods 5 and AirPods 4 don't.

**Case options.** The cheaper $129 AirPods 5 case charges by USB-C only. The $149 version adds Qi and Apple Watch charger support, a Find My speaker and volume swipe on the stems. If you lose things often, the speaker is worth the extra $20.

**Software features.** Live Translation, hands-free Siri and head gestures work on all three. Live Translation needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence.

## Which one should you buy?

- **Frequent flyer or daily commuter:** AirPods Pro 3. The noise cancellation and 8-hour battery are worth the extra money.
- **Gym and runners with an iPhone:** AirPods Pro 3 for the secure fit and heart rate tracking.
- **Office, home and casual listening:** AirPods 5. They're comfortable for hours and cost about $70 less than Pro 3 on sale.
- **Tight budget:** AirPods 5 at $129, or a clearance AirPods 4 with ANC if it's clearly cheaper. If you use Android, look beyond AirPods entirely; our [best noise cancelling headphones under $100](/best-noise-cancelling-headphones-under-100/) roundup is a good start.

AirPods 4 have been replaced, and AirPods 5 now give you noise cancellation for $129, so skip AirPods 4 unless the clearance price is clearly lower.
