# GadgetGyaan blog: notes for Claude

Before writing, planning or editing any post, read **WRITING_GUIDE.md**, starting with the section
"Quality first: NEW RULES" at the top (owner's decision, 25 Nov 2026). In short:

- At most 3 new posts a week (Mon, Wed, Fri IST). No new Launches or news posts; launches and news only update existing posts.
- Scheduled tasks never publish new posts. They write drafts to `_drafts_queue/<slug>.md` with 6–8 questions for the owner, Kitek Sood.
- A draft is published only after Kitek answers in Hindi, the answers are translated into his simple English, he approves them, and they are added as a big `## Our take` section.
- Never invent his experience, never write his Our take, never claim he wrote every word (AI helps with research and drafts; his opinions are his own).
- Pick topics he can add his own experience to. Prefer improving and merging existing posts over adding new ones.
- Never rename post files or change their dates. Never use Search Console from a scheduled task.
