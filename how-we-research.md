GadgetGyaan is run by **Kitek Sood**, a web and app developer based in India. This page explains exactly how our guides are put together, so you can decide how much to trust them.

## Where our facts come from

- **Specs and features** come from the brand's official product page or launch announcement. If a detail is only reported by news sites, we use at least two reliable ones.
- **Prices** are checked on Amazon.in and Flipkart (Amazon.com for Global posts) on the day we write or update a post. We write "around ₹X" with the month, because prices change every week.
- **Every post lists its sources** in a "Sources" box at the end, with links, so you can check them yourself.
- **Leaks and rumours are not facts.** If something is only rumoured, we say so or leave it out.

## What we test ourselves (and what we don't)

Most of our guides compare phones and gadgets using official specs, reliable reviews and current prices. **We don't claim to have tested a product unless we have.** When Kitek has used a product himself, his own view appears under **"Our take"** in the post.

Images on our posts are illustrations unless the caption says otherwise. We don't use brand product photos without permission.

## How we keep guides up to date

- When a price, launch date or software update changes, we update the post and show an **"Updated"** date at the top.
- Important changes get a short green **update note** explaining what changed.
- Our [Recent updates](/updates/) page lists every post we changed in the last 30 days.
- Our [Phone Finder](/phone-finder/) prices are refreshed every month.

## How we make money

Some links are affiliate links to Amazon and Flipkart. If you buy through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. **Commissions never decide what we recommend**, and we often suggest cheaper options. We don't accept paid reviews or paid links. Read our full [affiliate disclosure](/disclosure/).

## Found a mistake?

We'd rather fix an error quickly than leave it up. [Contact us](/contact/) with the post link and what's wrong, and we'll check it and correct the post, usually within 48 hours.
