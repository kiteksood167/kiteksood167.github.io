Quick tips before you buy

  • Don't chase megapixels. A 50MP camera with OIS (stabilisation) usually beats a 108MP camera without it.
  • Check update years. If you keep phones for 4–5 years, Samsung, Google and Apple promise the longest support.
  • AMOLED is worth it. If you watch a lot of videos, an AMOLED screen looks much better than LCD.
  • Wait for sale offers. Bank discounts often cut ₹2,000–₹5,000 off mid-range phones.
