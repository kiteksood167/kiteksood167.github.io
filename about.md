GadgetGyaan helps buyers make smarter choices about phones, laptops, earbuds and everyday tech. Most guides are written for India, and our **Global** section covers picks and prices for readers in the US, UK and elsewhere.

Tech shopping in India is confusing. Every week there's a new launch, every box is covered in big numbers, and sale banners make everything look like a deal. We cut through that noise with plain-English guides that explain **what actually matters** and **what is just marketing**.

## What we write about

- **Buying guides:** how to choose the right phone, laptop or earbuds for your budget
- **Explainers:** what specs like ANC, UFS storage or refresh rate really mean
- **Tips and how-tos:** getting more out of the gadgets you already own

## How we work

- India guides are written for **real Indian budgets and conditions**: sunlight, patchy networks, hot summers and sale-season prices.
- Global guides use **US dollar prices** and products that are widely available in the US and UK.
- We explain our reasoning so you can make your own decision.
- We update guides when things change.
- When we use affiliate links, we say so clearly. Commissions never decide what we recommend. Read our [affiliate disclosure](/disclosure/).
